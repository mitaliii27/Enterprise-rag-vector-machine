# Data Privacy Policy

## Retention
HR records are kept for 7 years after employment ends. Customer contact data is kept for 3 years after the last activity.

## Incidents and requests
Suspected personal data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours. The supervisory authority is notified within 72 hours where required. Data subject requests are answered within 30 days.

## AI tools
Personal data must not be entered into AI prompts unless a DPIA has approved the use case. Approved tools are listed on the intranet. Contact the DPO at privacy@northwind.example.
