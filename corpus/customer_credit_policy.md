# Customer Credit Policy

## Credit limits
New customers receive an initial credit limit of EUR 10,000. A formal credit check is required for any limit above EUR 5,000. The maximum payment term is net 45 days.

## Overdue accounts
Accounts 30 days overdue receive dunning level 1. Accounts 60 days overdue are placed on credit hold. Write-offs above EUR 5,000 require CFO approval.

## Disputes
Customer invoice disputes are resolved within 10 business days.
