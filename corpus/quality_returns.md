# Quality and Returns

## Customer returns
Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery and require an RMA number. A restocking fee of 15% applies to non-defective returns. Credit notes for defective goods are issued within 7 days of inspection.

## Nonconformance
Nonconformance reports are opened within 24 hours of detection. Supplier corrective action is requested within 5 days. An ISO 9001 surveillance audit is held every year.
