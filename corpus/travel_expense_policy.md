# Travel and Expense Policy

## Transport
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights over 6 hours with Department Head approval. Rail is preferred for journeys under 400 km.

## Accommodation and meals
Hotel stays are capped at EUR 180 per night in Tier 1 cities and EUR 120 per night elsewhere. The daily meal allowance is EUR 55. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Claims
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Receipts are required for any expense above EUR 25. Private car use is reimbursed at EUR 0.30 per km.
