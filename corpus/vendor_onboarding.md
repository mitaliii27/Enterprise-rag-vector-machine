# Vendor Onboarding

## Required documents
New vendors must provide a tax certificate and a bank letter. Critical suppliers must also hold ISO 9001 certification.

## Screening
Sanctions screening must be completed before a vendor is activated. Vendors are re-screened every year. Vendors are classified into risk tiers A, B and C.

## Service level and changes
Onboarding is completed within 5 business days of a complete submission. Any change to bank details requires call-back verification to a previously known phone number.
