# Delegation of Authority

## Approval matrix
Team Lead: up to EUR 5,000. Department Head: up to EUR 25,000. Vice President: up to EUR 100,000. CFO: up to EUR 500,000. Amounts above EUR 500,000 require CEO and Board approval.

## Contracts
Contracts above EUR 250,000 require legal review before signature. Contracts with auto-renewal clauses always require legal review regardless of value.

## Rules
Nobody may approve their own expenses or requisitions. Approval authority may be delegated during leave for a maximum of 30 days and the delegation must be recorded in writing.
