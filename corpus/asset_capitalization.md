# Asset Capitalisation

## Threshold
Items are capitalised when the purchase price is at least EUR 2,500. Items below the threshold are expensed.

## Useful lives
Straight-line depreciation is used with these useful lives: laptops: 3 years, vehicles: 5 years, machinery: 10 years, buildings: 40 years.

## Control
Assets under construction are transferred to fixed assets on commissioning. A physical verification of assets is performed once a year. Disposals require Finance approval.
