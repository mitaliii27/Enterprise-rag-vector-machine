# Month-End Close

## Calendar
Working day 1: sub-ledger cutoff at 18:00. Working day 3: intercompany reconciliation complete. Working day 5: final journal entries posted. Working day 6: financial statements delivered to the Controller.

## Accruals and journals
Accruals are required for any item above EUR 2,000. Journal entries above EUR 100,000 require Controller review. Late journals need an exception form signed by the Finance Director.
