# Procurement Policy

## Purchase requisitions
Catalogue items below EUR 1,000 are auto-approved. All other requisitions follow the Delegation of Authority matrix. Requisitions must name a cost centre and a business justification.

## Competitive quotes
Three competitive quotes are required for any purchase above EUR 25,000. Sole-source purchases need a written justification and Department Head approval.

## Blanket orders
Blanket purchase orders are valid for 12 months and must state a maximum total value. Call-offs against a blanket order do not need new quotes.

## Emergency purchases
Emergency purchases may be placed without prior approval to restore safety or production. They must be approved retroactively within 2 business days.
