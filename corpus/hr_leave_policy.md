# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year. A maximum of 5 days may be carried over into the next year. Leave of more than 10 consecutive days requires 4 weeks notice.

## Sickness
Employees may self-certify sickness for up to 3 days. A doctor's note is required afterwards.

## Parental leave
Paid parental leave is 16 weeks for each parent.
